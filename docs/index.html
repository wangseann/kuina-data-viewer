Requirements and update policy

Financial prices need at least two calendar years of actual history, hourly or finer native observations (15 minutes preferred), valid values and no unexplained empty expected hourly blocks under the correct calendar. Context retains its own native cadence and needs coverage and availability evidence.

No price observations, sentiment values, timestamp arrays, credentials, participant information or delegation tasks are published here. The data and task hub remain in private project storage.

Counts only change after an approved audit snapshot is published. If no new audit is available, the last-audit date stays unchanged.
